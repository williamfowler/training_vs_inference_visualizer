What this animation lies about

  • Scale. A frontier cluster has tens of thousands of GPUs, not 64. Everything here is one "pod" standing in for the whole.
  • No mixture-of-experts. MoE models add a large all-to-all exchange inside every layer, in both workloads. It is not shown.
  • Disaggregation is one design point. Colocated and chunked-prefill serving mix both phases on the same GPUs; those alternatives are not shown.
  • Stylized timings. Durations are tuned for legibility, not measured. Real prefills are milliseconds-to-seconds; real training steps vary widely.
  • TP as generic shimmer. Tensor-parallel traffic is really a precise per-layer sequence of all-reduces, not a continuous glow.
  • Fixed prefill→decode pairing. Real routers pick decode instances by load; here each prefill instance ships KV to a fixed partner for visual clarity.
  • No congestion. Links never queue, drop, or interfere. Real fabrics have contention, and topology-aware collectives exist to manage it.
  • Training shown un-overlapped. Real frameworks overlap gradient communication with the backward pass, which partially smears the heartbeat — it does not eliminate it.
